05 — Protocol Strengthening

Tighten the Science // critical fix

The methodological foundation (PRISMA, Cochrane) is solid. The threat is excessive parameter breadth collapsing the meta-analysis under heterogeneity.

Current weakness
Structural intervention domains include: policy environment, healthcare access, linkage to care, community empowerment, economic strengthening — and more. Pooling across all these in a single meta-analysis generates I² values that make results uninterpretable. Reviewers will note this.

The fix: Narrow to 2–3 domains maximum, pre-specified:

  • AHealth system delivery models (ART delivery integration, differentiated service delivery)
  • BSocial protection and economic strengthening programmes
  • CCommunity-based adherence and retention systems

Or: define a pre-specified stratified hierarchy of structural levels with subgroup analyses. The goal is comparing apples to apples. APOL1 status and HIV virological control status should be considered as effect modifiers in the subgroup framework.

ML advantage — name it
The Ukubona automated review infrastructure (ML-powered screening, LLM-assisted data extraction, vector-space search across citation networks) directly addresses the heterogeneity problem: it enables systematic sub-group analysis at a scale impossible with manual review. This is the methodological innovation that justifies U.S. presence and Hopkins partnership. Build it into the protocol narrative.
