04 — Protocol Strengthening

Tighten the Science // critical fix

The methodological foundation (PRISMA, Cochrane) is solid. The threat to the analysis is excessive parameter breadth.

Current weakness
Structural intervention domains currently include: policy environment, healthcare access, linkage to care, community empowerment, economic strengthening — and more. Pooling across all these domains in a meta-analysis will generate extreme heterogeneity. The statistical outputs will be uninterpretable.

The fix: Narrow to 2–3 domains maximum — for example:

  • AHealth system delivery models (ART delivery integration, differentiated service delivery)
  • BSocial protection and economic strengthening programmes
  • CCommunity-based adherence and retention systems

Alternatively, define a stratified hierarchy of structural levels and pre-specify subgroup analyses. The goal is ensuring you are comparing apples to apples, not apples to galaxies.
